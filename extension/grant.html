White Rabbit

Start with the past two days?

With your browser's history, Rabbit's first look can include the past two days, from before you added White Rabbit. Without it, Rabbit starts with what you browse from now on.

It reads a week back at most, on this computer, skipping incognito windows and the sites you exclude. Rabbit is sent page titles and sites from the past two days, never full addresses. You can take this back on your browser's extensions page.
